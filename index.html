About the club

What is PRUM?

PRUM is the People's Roleplaying Union of Mernone, a tabletop roleplaying club at VonKatLeeRes University's Clayton campus. We run weekly sessions across the most loved systems, host two camps a year, and send teams to inter-uni tournaments. No prior experience needed. Every Dungeon Master started as a confused first-time player too.

What happens at PRUM

Three ways to roll

Pick the cadence that fits your week. All three are open to new players, and you do not need to commit to all of them.

Weekly sessions

Every Thursday from 6pm. Dungeons & Dragons 5e, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, and whatever your GM is running. All levels welcome.

Thursdays · 6–10pm · Campus Centre

Annual camps

Two weekend camps per year in the Dandenongs. Bring a sleeping bag, leave with twenty new friends and a character who shouldn't have survived.

Autumn & Spring · Healesville Retreat

Inter-uni tournaments

We send teams to the Melbourne Tabletop League and the One-Shot Open. No experience required. We will teach you the system before the round.

Three events per year · Melbourne
